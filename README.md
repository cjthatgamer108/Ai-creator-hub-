# 🚀 AI Creator Growth Hub
Learn to use AI tools to grow your audience and monetize your content.

### 🛠 Essential AI Toolkit
* **Content Strategy:** [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) / [Claude](https://claude.ai)
* **Video/Shorts:** [InVideo AI](https://invideo.io) or [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com)
* **Thumbnails/Graphics:** [Canva AI](https://www.canva.com)

### 📈 How to get your first 1,000 views
1. **Identify a Niche:** Use AI to find trending topics in your area of interest.
2. **Batch Create:** Use Gemini/ChatGPT to write 10 scripts at once.
3. **AI Hooks:** Focus on the first 3 seconds of your video using high-retention AI templates.
